# Medicare design context

Medicare is a healthcare portal for patients and the clinical, administrative and support staff involved in their care. Users review records, visits, permissions and care workflows. The hosted free deployment contains synthetic demonstration data only.

The owner requested a conventional, professional healthcare login and then explicitly required the whole website to use the same theme. The implemented Medicare login is the reference: calm white and mint surfaces, restrained deep teal accents, Plus Jakarta Sans, generous but practical spacing, clear form labels and a custom Medicare icon. Preserve this direction rather than introducing another visual style.

Quality bar: consistent public, authentication and role-based screens in light and dark themes. Status colors communicate clinical/operational meaning; they do not change the brand by role. Functional behavior, authorization, consent, audit and demo limitations remain intact.
